GE Power

Boiler Performance Tools Modernization

Assignment: Technical Product Owner & Scrum Master

As Product Owner and Scrum Master, I led the team responsible for modernizing a portfolio of approximately 20 legacy FORTRAN engineering applications using C# and AWS. I owned requirements, backlog prioritization, sprint planning and coordination of business validation.

The Business Problem

Multiple engineering departments depended on custom legacy applications for boiler design and engineering work, some dating to the 1960s. They relied on aging FORTRAN software and unsupported infrastructure, so the business needed to preserve these engineering capabilities while moving to a more supportable technology environment. Protecting the intellectual property in externally licensed engineering software was also a business consideration.

Stakeholders

Business stakeholders, engineering users, external licensees, the solution architect, the software engineers and the cloud engineering team.

My Role

I worked with engineering stakeholders and software engineers to set modernization priorities, defined requirements and user stories with business stakeholders and the solution architect, owned and prioritized the backlog, planned the sprints, ran the Scrum ceremonies and coordinated business validation. I also supported the requirements for protecting proprietary engineering software.

Owned by others: The software engineers and solution architect owned the technical design and implementation, including the FORTRAN wrapper. The cloud engineering team provisioned the AWS environment.

Key Decisions and Tradeoffs

Prioritize by upcoming engineering work

With engineering stakeholders and the software engineers, I prioritized the applications needed for upcoming engineering projects.

Adapting to a conversion constraint

When engineers found that some modules could not be converted directly to C#, they designed a wrapper around the existing FORTRAN code. I worked with them to understand the impact, replaced the original conversion story with one for the wrapper work, had the team size it, and adjusted the sprint plan and priorities.

Validate with existing test cases

I worked with engineering application owners to identify their existing test cases and repurposed them to confirm the modernized applications produced equivalent calculation results. Business users validated releases in staging before production.

Business validation before release

I coordinated business-defined test cases and user acceptance testing in staging, so releases reached production only after business users had validated them.

Delivery and Validation

The team delivered the work in Scrum sprints from the backlog I owned, with business validation in staging before each production release.

Technical context

Angular user interface; C# application logic, with FORTRAN wrapped in C# where direct conversion wasn't possible; NuGet packaging; SQL Server; and AWS compute, managed database and private networking.

Results

Delivery

Multiple modernized applications were deployed to AWS, and engineers began using them. Approximately 20 applications were in the modernization scope.

Business continuity

Engineering capabilities supporting legacy boiler design were preserved while reducing dependency on unsupported technology.

Quality

Reusing existing engineering test cases gave the team a way to confirm that modernized applications produced equivalent calculation results.

Risk reduction

Intellectual property protection requirements for externally licensed engineering software were addressed, with hosted access giving the business greater control over its software.

Risk reduction

Moving external licensees to hosted access gave the business greater control over its software and intellectual property.

Intended benefit, not measured: lower infrastructure support costs by moving off aging infrastructure.

Lesson Learned

This experience reinforced the importance of identifying technical risks early, particularly when modernizing legacy applications. When engineering identifies a constraint, the Product Owner needs to understand the impact, evaluate alternatives with the team and adjust the backlog and delivery plan accordingly.
