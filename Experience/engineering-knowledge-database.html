Engineering Knowledge Database Replacement

Assignment: Product ownership, requirements discovery and phased delivery

I led the replacement of a retiring Lotus Notes knowledge database so engineering kept access to its documentation, building on the existing SharePoint platform and rolling out in phases.

The Business Problem

Engineering departments depended on years of accumulated documentation and procedures supporting boiler design. The retirement of Lotus Notes created a business continuity risk and a risk that engineers would rely on outdated information.

Stakeholders

Department leaders and engineers across approximately five engineering departments, starting with a smaller department, and individual engineers with specific needs.

My Role

I worked directly with department leaders and engineers to understand their business needs and define requirements. I owned the platform selection and rollout strategy, led the migration and prepared users for the transition. I also managed requirements prioritization and adjusted our approach based on user feedback.

Key Decisions and Tradeoffs

Reuse the existing platform

I evaluated existing enterprise capabilities before considering a new platform. SharePoint could provide the required functionality, and engineers were already familiar with it.

Pilot before expanding

I started with a smaller engineering department so we could learn and adjust before expanding the migration.

Don't invest in a retiring system

When an engineer asked for an enhancement to Lotus Notes, I kept the request in the backlog for the replacement instead of investing further in a system being retired.

Clean up metadata during migration

We used a third-party migration tool and process to transfer the documentation and remap its metadata, correcting outdated metadata along the way.

Add hands-on support

When early feedback showed that documentation alone wasn't enough, we added a week of lunchtime hands-on workshops.

Results

Delivery

Documentation for approximately five engineering departments was migrated, preserving years of engineering knowledge.

Adoption

Participating departments moved to the new solution and used it after migration and training.

Quality

Outdated metadata was corrected during the migration.

Cost

Avoided purchasing a replacement platform by using the existing SharePoint environment. A third-party migration tool was purchased for the migration.

Cost

Avoided purchasing a new platform by leveraging the existing SharePoint environment.

Lesson Learned

Delivering a technically sound solution is only part of the responsibility. We also need to prepare users, listen to their feedback and adjust our approach to support adoption.
