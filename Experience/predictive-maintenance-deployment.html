Predictive-Maintenance Application: AWS Deployment

Senior Staff Technical Program Manager · Assignment: Technical Product Manager

I took ownership of putting a purchased predictive-maintenance application into service on AWS for engineering users, from cross-team coordination to installation and user training.

The Business Problem

A purchased predictive-maintenance application needed an implementation owner to bring it into service. Purchased software often depends on cloud, security and architecture teams that were not part of the buying decision.

Stakeholders

Engineering users, enterprise architecture, cloud engineering, security and the vendor.

My Role

I took ownership of bringing the application into service. I coordinated requirements, approvals and the delivery approach across engineering users, enterprise architecture, cloud engineering, security and the vendor. I personally installed and configured the application, created environment-access documentation and a training video, and trained engineering users.

Owned by others: The cloud engineering team handled the AWS environment.

Delivery and Enablement

I worked with each team on its requirements and approvals, installed and configured the application on AWS with the cloud engineering team, and prepared engineers to use it through access documentation, a training video and hands-on training.

Results

Delivery

The application was deployed on AWS and made available to engineering users.

Quality

Required architecture and security approvals were in place before go-live.

Engineering users received access documentation, a training video and hands-on training.

Lesson Learned

Purchasing technology alone does not deliver business value. Clear implementation ownership, stakeholder alignment and user enablement are essential to putting that technology into operation.
