Application Portfolio Management

Senior Staff Technical Program Manager · Assignment: Application Portfolio Management Lead

I led application portfolio management for Steam IT, working with the CIO, directors, application owners and technical teams to improve portfolio visibility, address governance gaps and support technology investment decisions.

The Business Problem

When I took over application portfolio management, the Steam business needed to improve its application portfolio governance performance. Leadership also needed better visibility into portfolio health, composition and application retirement progress, and previously had to request that information from the application portfolio manager.

Stakeholders

The Steam CIO and IT directors, domain directors, application owners, the central portfolio governance team, and the technical-debt and security teams.

My Role

I worked with the CIO and directors to understand what information they needed to manage the application portfolio. I defined the dashboard requirements, built the Smartsheet tracking and reporting solution, worked with application owners to address information gaps and coordinated remediation activities with the responsible technical teams.

I also led quarterly portfolio reviews to help leadership understand application health, technical debt, hosting, modernization opportunities and decommissioning candidates.

Owned by others: The governance scoring methodology was defined centrally. Application owners made the final decision on retiring their applications, and application owners and technical teams made the improvements to them.

Key Decisions and Tradeoffs

Visibility first, accuracy in parallel

I didn't wait for every record to be perfect. I built the reporting view from the available data while working with owners to correct missing or inaccurate information, so the reporting and the source data improved together.

Targeted owner follow-up

When a group email drew too few responses, I moved to one-to-one conversations with the owners who had gaps.

A predictable cadence

I sent weekly updates to the central team so governance scores were recalculated as information improved, and I used the dashboard in quarterly reviews.

Solution and Delivery

I worked with the central governance team to understand the portfolio health metrics, then built a centralized Smartsheet dashboard from the enterprise source system. It covered portfolio health, technical debt, metadata completeness, application footprint, hosting distribution and application retirement progress.

I held quarterly reviews with application owners on whether each application was still needed and coordinated retirements while owners kept the final decision. I reported progress to leadership monthly, ran quarterly governance reviews with the Steam CIO and domain directors, and supported application decommissioning associated with a divestiture.

Results

Quality

Working with application owners and technical teams, we approximately doubled portfolio health scores within two quarters and met the centrally established governance targets for critical and noncritical applications. Some individual applications still had open issues.

Portfolio footprint

Over my ownership, the portfolio went from about 300 to about 100 applications, a reduction of approximately 67%. About three quarters of that reduction came from divestiture. About 50 applications were retired through rationalization outside the divestiture, with application owners making the final decisions.

Decision-making

The Steam CIO and IT directors gained self-service portfolio visibility, and the dashboard became the basis for quarterly governance discussions with leadership.

Accountability

A recurring governance process prompted remediation and retirement by application owners and technical teams.

Lesson Learned

Effective portfolio governance requires more than reporting. Application owners need to understand what is expected of them, and leadership needs reliable information to make decisions. Working directly with owners and technical teams helped us address gaps and improve the quality of the portfolio information.
