Application Portfolio Management

Senior Staff Technical Program Manager · Assignment: Application Portfolio Management Lead

I gave Steam IT leadership one view of its 200+ application portfolio, working with the CIO and directors on what to measure, and with application owners and technical teams to close governance gaps.

The Business Problem

When I took over application portfolio management for Steam IT, leadership didn't have a consolidated view of more than 200 applications. New centrally defined governance metrics required information that had not previously been captured.

Stakeholders

The CIO and directors, application owners, the central portfolio governance team, and the technical-debt and security teams.

My Role

I identified leadership's lack of portfolio visibility, worked with the CIO and directors to identify the dashboard metrics they needed, and built the tracking system and dashboard. I coordinated follow-up with application owners and technical teams, and I ran quarterly portfolio reviews.

Owned by others: The governance scoring methodology was defined centrally. Application owners and technical teams made the improvements to their applications.

Key Decisions and Tradeoffs

Visibility first, accuracy in parallel

I didn't wait for every record to be perfect. I built the reporting view from the available data while working with owners to correct missing or inaccurate information, so the reporting and the source data improved together.

Targeted owner follow-up

When a group email drew too few responses, I moved to one-to-one conversations with the owners who had gaps.

A predictable cadence

I sent weekly updates to the central team so governance scores were recalculated as information improved, and I used the dashboard in quarterly reviews.

Solution and Delivery

I built a Smartsheet tracking system and dashboard covering application health, technical debt, hosting, cloud candidates and decommissioning candidates, using exports from the central governance system.

Weekly updates kept the central scores current, and quarterly reviews brought CIOs, directors and application owners together around the same portfolio view.

Results

Quality

Working with application owners and technical teams, we helped the portfolio groups reach their centrally required governance targets in about two quarters. Some individual applications still had open issues.

Delivery

Leadership gained a consolidated portfolio view, used in quarterly reviews with CIOs, directors and application owners.

Cost

Decommissioning and cloud candidates, hosting information and related risks became visible, so leadership could prioritize cost-reduction work.

Lesson Learned

When governance requirements are new, pair a clear overall message with targeted support for the people who have specific gaps.
